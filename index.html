Good to know

FAQ

How long is a booking slot?

Slots are one hour. You can book consecutive hours if they’re available.

Do you rent paddles and balls?

Yes. Ask the desk on arrival. Gear rental can be added at check-in.

What’s the cancellation policy?

Cancel at least 4 hours before your slot for a full demo credit. Same-day no-shows are charged.

Is this a real booking system?

This build is a client demo with offline PWA support. Live payments and admin can plug in next.
